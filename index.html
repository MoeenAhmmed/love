The first anniversary is paper

I wrote you a letter

Tradition gives the first anniversary to paper, and the modern list gives it to clocks. I am giving you both: a letter, and every hour of the year ahead.

Dear Tasni,

One year ago I got to call you my wife. I thought I understood what that would mean. I didn't. I didn't know that a cup of tea, a long ride home, or a tired evening could become the best part of my day, just because you were there.

Thank you for your patience on my busiest days, for your kindness when I am not at my best, and for believing in me when I forget to. You make me better without ever asking me to be someone else.

I don't know everything the second year will bring. I know I want to meet all of it with you, the easy days and the hard ones, the loud laughs and the quiet evenings.

I love you more today than I did on our wedding day. Tomorrow, I will say the same.

Looking ahead

Year two, I promise

  • To choose you every day

    On the easy days and the hard ones, out loud and in the small things.

  • To listen first

    Before I fix, advise or explain, I will hear all of it.

  • To protect our slow time

    Tea, long talks, a walk with no destination. Work can wait.

  • To cheer louder

    Your goals are my goals. I will be the first one clapping.

  • To laugh every day

    Mostly with you, and sometimes at myself, so you don't have to.

  • To come home to you

    In every sense of the word, for every year after this one.
